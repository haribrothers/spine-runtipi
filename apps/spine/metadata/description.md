# Spine

Self-hosted business management for small service and project businesses: enquiry → quote → job → invoice → payment → books.

- **Quotes and jobs:** send quotes your customers accept online, turn them into jobs with milestones, variations, photos and costing.
- **Invoices and payments:** progress and milestone invoices, deposits and retention, payments matched to what they pay.
- **The books keep themselves:** every business event posts a balanced double-entry journal entry, so the profit and loss, balance sheet, aged receivables and tax reports are always up to date.
- **A field app** for your crew (Android), and an API and MCP server for your own tools and AI agents.

## Setting it up

- **Address for links:** the address you open Spine at, such as `https://spine.<your local domain>` with Runtipi's local subdomain, your own domain, or `http://<server IP>:7746`. Spine writes it into emails and PDFs, and accepts sign-in there.
- Open it and create your account: the first person to sign up owns the business. After that, people join by invitation.
